# Demo Script

Target duration: 5 minutes 20 seconds. Hackathon limit: 6 minutes.

## 0:00–0:35 — Problem and impact

“We accept privacy policies every day, but a summary does not explain what the policy means for a specific person. p7 turns difficult consent into evidence, impact, and actions.”

## 0:35–1:10 — Input and profile

Open p7, switch between English and Spanish, load the sample, select **Journalist**, and explain that this profile prioritizes location, contacts, and third-party sharing. Run the analysis.

## 1:10–2:00 — Index and data map

Show the personalized index, SHA-256 hash, clause count, and declared path from the person to the service, third parties, advertising, and AI models. State that the map reflects declarations rather than observed traffic.

## 2:00–3:00 — Evidence and gaps

Show two high-risk findings and their exact verified clauses. Compare a specified, ambiguous, and missing control. Explain that missing information does not prove a legal violation.

## 3:00–3:50 — Impact and informed consent

Read one location scenario and one contacts scenario, then complete two action items. Point out that consequences are labeled as possible scenarios.

## 3:50–4:35 — AI use and technical difference

“AI can help interpret, but it does not set the index. Go verifies the evidence and applies reproducible rules. This keeps the demo available without paid credits and isolates instructions hidden inside a document.”

Disclose that ChatGPT and Codex supported research, scope, architecture, coding, design, tests, and documentation.

## 4:35–5:05 — Learning and growth

Explain how the project extended prior backend and cybersecurity knowledge into explainable AI for non-technical users. Mention separating evidence, interpretation, and scenarios; deterministic scoring; untrusted input; and user-focused scope.

Personalize this paragraph before recording so it accurately represents your prior experience.

## 5:05–5:20 — Next steps

Close with the evaluation dataset, user testing, optional semantic classifier, version comparison, and verified Latin American legal sources.
