# Five-Person Usability Study — Ready to Run

Status: prepared; no participants have been recruited or tested yet. Do not report this protocol as user feedback.

## Participants and consent

Invite five adults or eligible students with varied privacy and technical familiarity. Avoid collecting names, contact details, policy texts from personal accounts, or screen recordings without separate consent. Assign P1–P5. Explain that the tool is informational, participation is voluntary, and notes will be anonymous. Use the synthetic example policy.

## Session, 10–12 minutes each

1. Before showing p7, ask: “What do you think this service does with your data?” Record a short answer.
2. Ask the participant to load the example, select a profile, and run an analysis without coaching. Record whether they complete it and where they hesitate.
3. Ask: “Which finding concerns you most, and what sentence in the policy supports it?” Record whether they locate the evidence.
4. Ask: “Does the map show actual network traffic or what the policy declares?” Record the answer.
5. Ask them to find one missing or ambiguous disclosure and choose one action they would take. Record their reasoning.
6. Ask for a confidence rating from 1–5 and one unclear phrase or interface element.
