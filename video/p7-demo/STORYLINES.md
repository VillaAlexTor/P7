# Storylines

## Recommended — Evidence Journey

Device and form: one real policy travels through p7 from unreadable text to evidence, gaps, impact, and action; real product screens fill the frame while the camera follows the work. The viewer first feels the weight of an unread policy, then watches p7 turn it into a decision they can verify. The first two seconds show dense policy text immediately replaced by the question, “What are you accepting?” Beats: policy wall → paste and profile → score and declared map → verified clauses → gaps → actions → honest AI evaluation → human-review conclusion. Look: p7’s warm paper ground, black editorial type, red-orange accent, full-size real UI. Sound: calm documentary English narration, restrained electronic pulse, quiet clicks only on visible actions.

## Alternative — The Confidence Trap

Device and form: start with a confident AI answer, break it apart with the twelve denial-clause failure, and rebuild trust through evidence. The film feels investigative and technical, with sharp match cuts between model output, verified clauses, and evaluation numbers. First two seconds: “CONFIDENT” lands, then is stamped “WRONG.” Beats: confident output → failure → evidence gate → product flow → complete-policy result → human review. Look: higher contrast, more red accent, evaluation-led. Sound: tense minimal pulse that opens when evidence appears.

## Alternative — Follow Your Data

Device and form: the declared data path acts as a continuous line carrying the camera through the whole product. The viewer follows one piece of data from the user to the service, third parties, advertising, and AI training, then back to an action they can take. First two seconds: a small “you” node sends one line into a dense policy. Beats: person → policy → map → findings → possible impact → action → end. Look: spacious diagram-led compositions using the real map and interface. Sound: lighter investigative rhythm with small directional effects.

The recommended storyline is selected because it proves the working product first and uses the evaluation failure as honest supporting evidence rather than making the whole film about a mistake.
