# Final review — p7 STEM2Connect demo

## Creative intent

The video follows an evidence journey: a difficult privacy policy becomes a traceable finding, a practical impact scenario, and a concrete action. A later chapter resolves the name p7 into all seven product pillars and lands on the real Spanish interface. The editorial cream, black, red, and green palette mirrors the product screenshots and keeps the result serious without feeling institutional.

The central promise is intentionally narrow and credible: p7 helps people understand declared privacy practices before accepting them. It does not claim to observe network traffic, prove legal compliance, or replace professional advice.

## What was verified

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, 3:48.6 runtime.
- Real p7 interface captures are used throughout the product demonstration.
- The demonstrated run is internally consistent: Journalist produces four findings and a score of 97; switching to General produces a score of 80 over the same text.
- The Analyze action, its loading state, the profile change, and the second result are visible on screen.
- The evaluation chapter identifies 54.5%/60.0% as an early baseline and 87.5% precision, 70.0% recall, and 0/4 exact pages as the latest documented round.
- HyperFrames lint, runtime, audio, contrast, and layout blocker checks passed with zero blocking errors.
- Automated contrast checks passed 34/34.
- The original eighteen representative beats and all eight scene seams were visually reviewed; the added seven-pillar scene and its outgoing seam were reviewed separately at four beats and five seam frames.
- The rendered MP4 was decoded and spot-checked at the hook, evidence, AI architecture, seven-pillar chapter, and closing sections.
- English narration and background music are present for the full program.
- The delivery file uses H.264 video and 48 kHz AAC audio. Final audio measures -16.2 LUFS integrated with a -1.5 dBFS true peak.
- English subtitles are delivered as a separate SRT file.

## Deliberate choices and retained notices

The opening policy fragments intentionally move beyond the canvas and sit beneath a shade layer; they are atmosphere rather than required reading. Several scenes hold longer than three seconds so judges can inspect the real interface. Those intentional holds produced non-blocking craft notices in the automated review.

The weakest shot in the first pass was the AI architecture card: its final label sat too close to the lower edge. The node sizing and padding were adjusted before the final render. During the later alignment review, the main omission was that the seventh pillar—privacy for everyone—was only implied. A dedicated chapter now names all seven pillars and explicitly connects the final one to bilingual, plain-language access and Latin American communities.

## Audio note

Audio was measured programmatically for loudness, peak safety, duration, and stream integrity. The normalized delivery preserves the rendered video stream without recompression. It was not auditioned through human ears in this environment.
